Shape (0x30 bytes)

u32 type, then 11 words. Types 3 and 6 hold an offset to an embedded COL mesh at 0x24 (type 3) or 0x28 (type 6), which the loader opens with 0x8008ef70. Types 4 and 5 are attached to a bone: the bone's name hash at 0x18 (type 4) or 0x24 (type 5) is replaced at load by the bone's index in the model's LOD 0 skeleton, or -1 (0x800f33b0), and the centre at 0x04–0x10 follows that bone.

Collision Surfaces (.csd)

CollisionSurfaces.csd in global.wad. Loader: 0x8008ee90.

OffsetSizeTypeDescription
0x004char[4]Magic STD
0x044u32Version; must be 6
0x084u32Surface count; 0 makes the game use a blank table
0x0C-Surfaces, 0x10 bytes each

Each surface is a u32 name hash, a u32 flags, and two u32 hashes. The game finds a surface by binary search on the name hash (0x8008f030), so the table is sorted by it; a hash it can't find is logged once and treated as surface 0. The search returns the low 16 bits of the flags and the index. The two hashes at 0x08 and 0x0C are what a kart reads for the surface it touches (0x8006d3b0, 0x8006d3e0); the first selects the surface's handling (0x80026c60 switches on it).

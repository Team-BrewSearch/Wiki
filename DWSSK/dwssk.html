DreamWorks Super Star Kartz on PlayStation 3, Xbox 360, Wii and 3DS stores its assets in HIG! WAD containers whose entries are typed and found by path hash. The DS version runs on a different engine and is covered under Virtuos DS Racing Engine. Layouts here come from the game's own code on all four platforms (addresses are from the Wii executable) and are checked against OmegaStudio's DWSSK plugin.

File Formats

    Conventions

    Byte order
    Big-endian on PS3, Xbox 360 and Wii; little-endian on 3DS. WAD headers and entry headers are little-endian everywhere.
    off
    A byte offset from the start of the file or structure; 0 means none
    Runtime
    A field the game overwrites while loading. The files hold whatever the export tools left there, and nothing reads it.
    Units
    World positions in .gdw, .gdi and .gdt are centimetres (drawn at 0.01). .gds bone translations are centimetres scaled by 1/800.
    Vertex colours
    A, R, G, B on Xbox 360 (its D3DCOLOR); R, G, B, A everywhere else

    Data Layout

    • Each level is a WAD in up to three parts, .wad, .wad.mem1 and .wad.vram, loaded together.
    • global.wad holds shared assets, such as CollisionSurfaces.csd and textures many levels use.
    • Characters keep their models in a MASTER folder with the .animpack files beside it.
    • Sound banks (.bnk), Wwise audio (.wem), Scaleform movies (.swf), Bink videos (.bik) and level name tables (.nog) sit outside the WADs. The first four are standard formats and have no pages here.
    Verification: OmegaStudio's skinning palettes match the Wii palette builder (0x800cab00) bit for bit across 10 characters and 30 random clips, and its animation decoders match the game's on all 1,748,046 key values in six packs. Its quaternion-to-matrix routine matches System.Numerics to 2e-8.

    In OmegaStudio

    • A level's WAD parts open as one tree. A texture a model names is looked up in its own WAD, then its other parts, then the global.wad in a folder above, and last in the other WADs of the game folder, indexed the first time a texture is missing.
    • WADs, sound banks, movies, videos and name tables also open inside PS3, Xbox 360, Wii and 3DS disc images, where a WAD row decompresses the first time it is expanded and edits go back to the disc when it allows them.
    • Textures decode for every platform and are replaced from PNG. Models render with their textures, skinned models play every clip in their .animpack files, and geometry is replaced from glTF.
    • Level databases, tuning tables, strings, fonts, cameras, collision, collision surfaces, navigation meshes and name tables export and import as JSON. Navigation and collision show in the Viewport and export as OBJ; level placements export as a glTF scene.
    • Shaders export as JSON with their disassembly. Audio decodes inside the plugin (PCM, Wwise IMA, DSP ADPCM, Wwise Vorbis, XMA2) and is replaced from WAV in its own codec; Bink 1 videos play with sound.
    • Every WAD, U8 archive and sound bank extracts to a folder with a manifest and rebuilds from it. TJZIP compression is rebuilt byte for byte, so an untouched WAD saves to the same file.

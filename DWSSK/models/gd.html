Model formats share a 0x50-byte header with materials, textures and shaders, then branch by kind. .gdw is a world mesh, .gdi an instanced mesh, .gdt and .gdt.piece are tie meshes, and .gds is a skinned mesh that also carries its skeleton. Data starts at 0x50 in every file.

Textures and Shaders

A model names its textures by path hash and its shaders by path hash. The loader turns the texture hash into a handle. Shaders are vertex/pixel shader pairs named by path hash; the loader makes one program per shader entry, and material passes name a program by its index.

Materials

A material is 0xAC bytes on the Wii, 0xC0 on the PS3 and Xbox 360, and 0x8C on the 3DS. The files set flag bits 0x1, 0x2 and 0x4; the loader adds 0x10 when any parameter scrolls and 0x20 when a texture record uses the reflection callback.

Units

Positions are drawn with a uniform scale that differs by kind:

  • .gdw, .gdi and .gdt: 0.01 — centimetres.
  • .gds: the float at mesh + 0x04, which is 8.0 in every file. Bone translations are centimetres scaled by 1/800 into the same space.

The header's bounding radius at 0x40 is in metres, after these scales.

A batch is a u16 material index, a u16 zone count, and an offset to zones (0x10 bytes each). Visibility Tree Node (0x2C bytes)

f32[3] bounding box minimum, f32[3] bounding box maximum, a u32 at 0x18 (not read by the walk), and four child offsets at 0x1C (0 = none). The walk tests each box, records inside/outside/partial per node, and gives a whole subtree the parent's answer when the parent is entirely in or out.

LOD, Xbox 360 and 3DS (0x14 bytes)

Five draw-list offsets; list k runs from entry k to entry k + 1.

LOD, PS3 (0x74 bytes)

Five draw-list offsets at 0x00, then four groups at 0x14. Each group is 0x18 bytes: a u32 skinned vertex count, a u32 rigid vertex count, an offset to skinned vertices (80 bytes each), an offset to rigid vertices (64 bytes each), then two runtime vertex buffers.

Vertex Formats

The Wii describes its formats with GXSetVtxAttrFmt; the PS3, Xbox 360 and 3DS with named declaration tables.

Wii
FormatUsed byPositionNormalColourUV
3.gdt, .gdw (24 bytes: pos 0, norm 12, colour 16, UV 20)f32 × 3s8 × 3, /64RGBA8s16 × 2, /1024
4.gdss16 × 3, /16384s8 × 3, /64RGBA8s16 × 2, /1024

Every Wii mesh is indexed: positions, normals, colours and UVs each take a u16 index. .gdt and .gdw point all four arrays into one 24-byte vertex, so the four indices of a vertex are always equal.

Wii skinned vertex (32 bytes): s16 position × 3 (/16384, GQR6) at 0, s8 normal × 3 (/64, GQR7) at 6, 3 bytes unused, four joint bytes at 0x0C (top bit ignored), four f32 weights at 0x10.

Wii rigid vertex (10 bytes): the same position and normal.

.gdi vertex (36 bytes): f32 position, f32 UV at 12, f32 normal at 20, RGBA8 colour at 32.

Colours are D3DCOLOR: stored A, R, G, B on Xbox 360 and R, G, B, A on PS3. The PS3's skinned stride leaves bytes 72–79 unused. Blend indices select from the draw's palette on the Xbox 360 and index the skeleton directly on the PS3.

3DS

Static, 24 bytes: f32 position × 3 at 0, s16 UV × 2 (/1024) at 12, normal at 16 (u8 × 3 as (n − 128) / 127 then a pad byte), RGBA8 colour at 20. The skinned vertex (32 bytes) adds four joint bytes at 24 and four weight bytes at 28.

Wii Display Lists

GX commands padded with zeros to 32 bytes. 0x98 draws a triangle strip, 0x90 a triangle list and 0xA0 a fan, followed by a big-endian u16 vertex count and one u16 index per enabled attribute per vertex, in GX order: position, normal, colour, UV. The files use 0x9B (format 3) for .gdt and .gdw and 0x9C (format 4) for .gds.

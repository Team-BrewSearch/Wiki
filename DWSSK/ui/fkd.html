Glyph (8 bytes)

Glyphs follow the ranges in order, one per code: u16 x, u16 y, u16 advance, u16 height. The loader keeps a pointer to the glyphs at 0x38 and rejects a font whose bytes 0x04–0x06 or 0x0D are wrong.

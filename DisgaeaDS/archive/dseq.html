DSEQ packs one piece of music into a .msnd file: an SSEQ sequence, the SBNK bank it plays through, and the SWAR of samples that bank uses. Nintendo's SDKs keep these in an SDAT; Disgaea DS loads the three directly.

Header

OffsetSizeTypeDescription
0x004char[4]Magic "DSEQ"
0x0412u8[12]0
0x104u32SSEQ offset plus 0x10
0x144u32SBNK offset
0x184u32SWAR offset
0x1C4u320
0x204u32SSEQ size
0x244u32SBNK size
0x284u32SWAR size
0x2C4u32Unknown; possibly the high half of the SWAR size

Offsets count from the start of the file; subtract 0x10 from the word at 0x10 to get the SSEQ's. The three chunks always come in the order SSEQ, SBNK, SWAR, each directly after the last.

SBNK files map instrument patches to a group of sound files in SWAV archives. Think of them as a lightweight SoundFont or a DirectWave bank in FL Studio.

In Disgaea DS, SBNK files link all music sequences to their samples. They must be paired with a SWAR to resolve sample references - the game's code handles this directly, unlike the standard Nintendo SDK SDAT approach.

SBNK files are only found inside DSEQ files, which are stored in DSARCFL archives.

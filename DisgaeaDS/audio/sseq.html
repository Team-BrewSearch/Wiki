SSEQ is Nintendo's DS sequence format: a compiled stream of note and control commands, usually built from a text source. All music in Disgaea DS is SSEQ. A sequence plays through an SBNK and its SWAR, and all three travel together in a DSEQ file.

Specifications

  • NitroStudio2: SSEQ
  • FeShrine: SDAT and SSEQ
  • TCRF: SDAT and SSEQ

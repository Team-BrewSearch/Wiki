SSEQ

Sequenced instruction format for music

sequence SSEQ is a sequenced instruction format, typically compiled from an ASCII tracker-like source. In Disgaea DS, all music uses SSEQ.

For SSEQ to play sound, it must be linked to an SBNK and a SWAR. SSEQ files are only found inside DSEQ files stored in DSARCFL archives.

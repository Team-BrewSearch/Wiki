STRM

Nintendo DS streamed audio

stream STRM is a chunk-encoded audio format from the Nintendo DS SDK. It appears in about half of all DS games due to its good quality-to-size ratio.

In Disgaea DS, developers use .strm files for sound effects, not music - and they use .strm and .swav interchangeably despite being different formats. See SWAV for the wave format.

SWAV is Nintendo's DS single-sample format: one PCM8, PCM16 or IMA-ADPCM wave with its rate and loop point. In Disgaea DS sound effects and voice clips in se.dat and voice.dat are SWAV, stored under both .se and .swav names; the four yrah_.swav files in se.dat are empty.

Specifications

  • NitroStudio2: SWAV
  • GBATEK: SWAV
  • OpenKH: SWAV

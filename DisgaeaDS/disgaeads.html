File formats of Nippon Ichi Software's Disgaea DS. The game keeps most of its data in two archive types, DSARC FL and DSARCIDX, and its music in Nintendo's standard SDAT formats wrapped in a custom container.

Sources: OmegaStudio has no Disgaea plugin, so nothing here is checked against it. DSARC FL and DSEQ are documented from the retail ROM; the other pages point to the external specifications listed on each.

    In OmegaStudio

    The Nintendo plugin opens the ROM itself: every file in it can be extracted or replaced, and the header and banner can be edited. The archives and formats on these pages show as raw files.

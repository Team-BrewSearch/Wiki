bgm.bin, se.bin and voice.bin are ordinary BIN archives. Their leaves are standard audio files, so no custom decoder is needed: extract the blob, write it out, play it.

Payload Identification

FormatMagicBytes
Ogg Vorbis"OggS" at offset 04F 67 67 53
RIFF WAVE"RIFF" at offset 0, "WAVE" at offset 852 49 46 46

Those two are the only audio formats present in the retail data. The game ships ogg.dll and vorbis.dll and decodes Vorbis through them.

bgm.bin, se.bin and voice.bin are ordinary BIN archives whose leaves are standard audio files. There is no custom container or codec: extract a leaf and any player opens it.

Payload Identification

FormatMagicBytesUsed by
Ogg Vorbis"OggS" at 04F 67 67 53bgm.bin
RIFF WAVE"RIFF" at 0, "WAVE" at 852 49 46 46se.bin, voice.bin

These are the only audio formats in the retail data. The game ships ogg.dll and vorbis.dll and decodes Vorbis through them.

In OmegaStudio

The Standard Formats plugin recognises both payloads inside the archive, so they play in the Audio pane and extract as .ogg or .wav. The archive walk stops at them rather than reading them as tables.

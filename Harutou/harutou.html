Suzumiya Haruhi no Chourantou, best known in its updated release Suzumiya Haruhi no Gekitou, is a 2007 Direct3D 9 game for Windows. These pages cover its data/*.bin archives and the assets inside them. Every layout here is checked against OmegaStudio's Harugeki plugin.

File Formats

    Conventions

    Byte order
    Little-endian
    Floats
    IEEE-754 binary32
    Strings
    Fixed-width ASCII, null padded, not always null terminated
    Matrices
    4x4, row-vector Direct3D convention (v' = v * M), translation in the last row
    Axes
    Y up, roughly 1 unit per centimetre
    Padding
    0xFE inside archive tables, 0x00 after payloads

    Geometry, bind matrices and animation keys all share one space. OmegaStudio brings it into glTF space by negating X and Z on every position and normal and conjugating every matrix by diag(-1, 1, -1, 1). Triangle order is kept as stored.

    Data Layout

    Every .bin under data/ is the same nested table of contents. Leaves carry no names, so the labels in parentheses below describe what a payload is; nothing in the file stores them.

    data/ ├── *.bin [BIN] - character and stage archives │ ├── (model) - node graph, meshes, embedded ringtex2 textures │ └── (animations) - one clip each, siblings of the model ├── bgm.bin [BIN] │ └── (Ogg Vorbis) - background music ├── se.bin [BIN] │ └── (RIFF WAVE) - sound effects ├── voice.bin [BIN] │ └── (RIFF WAVE) - voice clips ├── eff.bin [BIN] │ └── (BTEF) - 29 effect scripts, not decoded ├── param.bin - gameplay tables, not decoded ├── sce.bin - not decoded ├── sys.bin - not decoded └── sel.cdt - UI data, not decoded

    In OmegaStudio

    • Any data/*.bin whose root passes the table check opens as a tree. Models, their meshes, materials, skeleton and embedded textures, and animation clips all appear as rows.
    • A model plays every clip that sits beside it in the same table.
    • A texture is replaced from a PNG of any size. An embedded texture is spliced back into its model, and the model's offsets are moved to match.
    • A model's geometry is replaced from a glTF file, with joints bound to bones by name. See Model.
    • Any slot can be extracted or replaced as raw bytes, and Ogg and WAV leaves play in the Audio pane.
    • Saving rebuilds the whole archive in place.

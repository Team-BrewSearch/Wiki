glTF Conventions

  • A node or mesh named chunk_<n> goes to chunk, model or part n. Without the name, track triangles go to the nearest chunk position and everything else goes to the first chunk.
  • A joint named bone<N> binds to skeleton bone N.
  • OmegaStudio's exports store each primitive's block list, texcoord flags and group breaks in its extras as virtuosBlocks, virtuosTexcoordEmit and virtuosGroupBreaks (kartz* also reads), so an unedited model rebuilds with the strips it came with.
  • UVs are normalised on export and scaled back to texels by the bound texture's size on import.

Editing a Track

The .bpvs and the TRK tail sections are keyed by chunk index, not by geometry. They stay valid only while a track keeps its chunk count, its primary and detail split and each chunk's position. A different layout breaks both: a chunk 5 that covers a different part of the world than the original chunk 5 gives per-chunk visual corruption and rasteriser crashes. That is why a track import keeps the original layout.

The .col is not regenerated when a track changes. Replace it separately; the writer rebuilds what it can derive from the geometry, copies the rest, and never rewrites the sector distance chain, which accumulates in link order rather than table order.

Known Limits

  • Monster Jam tracks cannot be written back. Their chunks use the wide header, and what places a wide chunk, rather than a position from the table, is not worked out. Reading, drawing and exporting work.
  • Monster Jam ships no collision or visibility: its 29 .col files are zero bytes and its 29 .bpvs files are 12-byte stubs.
  • Nine files come back reordered from glTF: three Hot Wheels tracks, and six scenery models (one from Madagascar Kartz, one each from Hot Wheels and Pimp My Ride, three from Monster Jam).
  • Six Monster Jam scenery models lose up to 24 units of position accuracy when rebuilt.
  • Six of the 49 Kartz .amd files differ after a round trip by at most 0.0014 units, from 1/4096 grid rounding through the rest pose on the deepest bone chains.
  • The 12 bytes an .amd keeps per part, and the 12 bytes per model in a .zon trailer, are not decoded and are copied through unchanged.
  • In a Pimp My Ride chunk with prefix words, the reader and writer count texture reference offsets from different points (see TRK).

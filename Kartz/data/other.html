Three kinds of file sit in the archive that nothing here decodes. Each is load-bearing for the running game, and an edited track leaves them describing the geometry it replaced.

Status: Not reverse-engineered.

Files

FileNotes
.colPer-track collision mesh, beside the .trk. Element 0 of the track's model list in the RDF
.bpvsVisibility and occlusion data, keyed by chunk index. Element 2 of the same model list. Its section count matches the track's primary chunk count on 10 of 16 Super Star Kartz tracks and is exactly one higher on the other 6
.zonZone and prop model bundles, in the lower-case track directory - for example sharkbeach/water.zon. Each is named by the model property of a dynamicobject record in the RDF, which also carries that object's own texture table, so a .zon's textures do not compete for the track's slots

Editing a Track

.bpvs and the TRK tail sections are keyed by chunk index rather than by geometry, so they stay valid as long as a track keeps its chunk count, its primary and detail split, and its per-index positions. A different chunk layout invalidates both: a chunk 5 covering a different world region than the original chunk 5 produces per-chunk visual corruption and rasterizer crashes.

.col is not keyed that way and cannot be sidestepped: after a geometry change the collision mesh still describes the original track.

Reference for the file formats used by the Virtuos DS Racing Game Engine on Nintendo DS, reverse engineered from the retail archives. The engine ships in Madagascar Kartz, DreamWorks Super Star Kartz, Hot Wheels: Battle Force 5, Pimp My Ride: Street Racing and Monster Jam: Path of Destruction. The two Kartz games are the detailed reference pair on most pages. The other three use the same formats with the header and record variants listed under Engine coverage.

File Formats

    Conventions

    These hold across every format on this wiki unless a page says otherwise.

    Byte order
    Little-endian for all integers
    World positions
    20.12 signed fixed point (value * 4096)
    Texture coordinates
    12.4 signed fixed point (value * 16)
    Colours
    RGB555, bit 15 unused in palettes
    Geometry
    Pre-built Nintendo DS GX display list commands
    Paths
    Null-terminated ASCII, backslash-separated
    Transparency
    Magenta colorkey, RGB (255, 0, 255)

    Data Layout

    Each game carries one GDA/GDF/GDT archive triple under Publish.NDS/ and the resource databases that record the order those assets load in. The archive stem varies by game. The two Kartz examples are DWK.gda, DWK.gdf and DWK.gdt for Super Star Kartz, with 1770 entries, and MAD.gda, MAD.gdf and MAD.gdt for Madagascar Kartz, with 1377 entries.

    Publish.NDS/ ├── <archive>.gda - archive data ├── <archive>.gdf - path table ├── <archive>.gdt - offset/size table ├── main.rdf [RDF] - resource database ├── ingamemenu.rdf, menupages.rdf [RDF] - Super Star Kartz only ├── maintrack<track>.rdf [RDF] - one per track, Super Star Kartz only └── *.raf [RAF] - one per .rdf, its pointer fixup table

    Entries inside the archive are addressed by path, one per track directory:

    Models\Tracks\FarFarAway\ ├── FarFarAway.trk [TRK] - track geometry ├── FarFarAway.col [COL] - collision surface ├── FarFarAway.bpvs [BPVS] - chunk visibility list ├── *.zon [ZON] - scenery bundles └── *.5tx [5TX] - the track's own textures models\tracks\farfaraway\ └── *.zon [ZON] - scenery bundles in the lower-case directory

    Nothing in the archive records which files belong to a track. The RDF does: a track's model list names its .col, .trk and .bpvs by archive index, and its texture table gives the slot each .5tx occupies.

    Engine Coverage

    All five games share one engine and one set of formats. The later games move fields rather than change what a file means. The plugin measures these variants per file instead of hardcoding one layout.

    FormatVariants across the five games
    .trkMadagascar Kartz and Super Star Kartz keep the chunk table offset at +0x0C (0x38), Pimp My Ride keeps it at +0x0C (0x20), and Hot Wheels keeps it at +0x1C. Monster Jam keeps a u32 chunk count at +0x00, the table at +0x04 and the table end at +0x08.
    .colCross-section pointers sit at +0x60/+0x64 in the two Kartz games, +0x58/+0x5C in Pimp My Ride and +0x7C/+0x80 in Hot Wheels. A record runs to the next one rather than to a fixed length, so Hot Wheels fits records of 136 bytes and more against the 112 and 96 of the others.
    .skeA bone record is 68 bytes or 132 bytes. The first games store the step from the parent and chain it; the later games store the placed bone and the transform back into its parent. Reading either gives the same rest pose and writing one back keeps its shape.
    .aniThe header is 8 bytes or 20 bytes. The wide header counts in 32 bits, states where the last track ends, and pads gaps between frames with zero words.
    .amdThe Kartz games draw a character from one chunk that names a bone per vertex. Hot Wheels splits a character into parts, each an eight-byte record naming its bone and pointing at its own chunk.
    .carThe chunk table sits at +0x10 or +0x14. Later games leave gaps between chunks where the first games butt them together.
    Chunk headerA chunk header is 24 bytes or 108 bytes, with 12-byte or 16-byte texture references. The wide header used by Monster Jam carries a bounding box between the common fields and the references. A narrow chunk can also carry one or two of its own words before the display list, in which case those words count as part of the display list size.
    .zonThe header is 12 bytes or 16 bytes, the later one carrying a spare zero word.

    The detailed field layouts, per-format counts and known limits are in the decoded-assets index.

    Verified: across all five games the plugin reads 86 .col, 588 .zon, 115 .bpvs, 86 .trk, 103 .car, 64 .amd, 43 .ske and 348 .ani files. The 29 .col and 29 .bpvs files Monster Jam ships empty are the only ones with nothing to read. Every file that is read parses and re-serialises to the bytes it was read from.

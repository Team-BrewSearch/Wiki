Virtuos built one racing engine for five Nintendo DS games: Madagascar Kartz, DreamWorks Super Star Kartz, Hot Wheels: Battle Force 5, Pimp My Ride: Street Racing and Monster Jam: Path of Destruction. All five share one set of formats. The two Kartz games are the reference pair most measurements come from; the other three move fields around, as listed under Engine Variants. Every layout here is checked against OmegaStudio's Virtuos plugin.

File Formats

    Conventions

    Byte order
    Little-endian
    World positions
    20.12 signed fixed point (value * 4096)
    Texture coordinates
    12.4 signed fixed point (value * 16), in texels
    Colours
    RGB555: red in bits 0-4, green 5-9, blue 10-14
    Geometry
    Pre-built Nintendo DS GX display lists
    Paths
    Null-terminated ASCII, backslash-separated
    Transparency
    Magenta colour key, RGB (255, 0, 255)
    Up axis
    Z in skeletons and animation; OmegaStudio turns it to Y

    Data Layout

    Each game carries one GDA/GDF/GDT archive triple under Publish.NDS/, plus the resource databases that say how its files fit together. The archive stem varies by game: Super Star Kartz ships DWK.gda with 1770 entries, Madagascar Kartz MAD.gda with 1377.

    Publish.NDS/ ├── <stem>.gda - archive data ├── <stem>.gdf - path table ├── <stem>.gdt - offset and size table ├── main.rdf [RDF] - resource database ├── ingamemenu.rdf, menupages.rdf [RDF] - Super Star Kartz only ├── maintrack<track>.rdf [RDF] - one per track, Super Star Kartz only └── *.raf [RAF] - one per .rdf, its pointer fixup table

    Inside the archive, each track has a directory of its own:

    Models\Tracks\FarFarAway\ ├── FarFarAway.trk [TRK] - track geometry ├── FarFarAway.col [COL] - collision surface ├── FarFarAway.bpvs [BPVS] - chunk visibility ├── *.zon [ZON] - scenery bundles └── *.5tx [5TX] - the track's textures

    Nothing in the archive records which files belong to a track. The RDF does: a track's model list names its .col, .trk and .bpvs by archive index, and its texture table gives the slot each .5tx fills.

    Engine Variants

    The later games move fields rather than change what a file means. OmegaStudio detects each variant per file instead of assuming one layout.

    FormatVariants across the five games
    .trkChunk table field at +0x0C (value 0x38 in the Kartz games, 0x20 in Pimp My Ride) or +0x1C (Hot Wheels). Monster Jam stores a u32 chunk count at +0x00, the table at +0x04 and the table end at +0x08.
    ChunkNarrow 24-byte header with 12-byte texture references, or wide 108-byte header with 16-byte references (Monster Jam). A narrow chunk can carry prefix words before its display list (Pimp My Ride).
    .colCross-section pointers at +0x60/+0x64 (Kartz), +0x58/+0x5C (Pimp My Ride) or +0x7C/+0x80 (Hot Wheels). Sector records are 112, 96 or 136+ bytes.
    .zon12-byte header, or 16 bytes with a spare zero word.
    .carChunk table field at +0x10 or +0x14. Later games leave gaps between chunks.
    .amdOne chunk with a bone per vertex (Kartz), or parts that each hang from one bone (Hot Wheels).
    .ske68-byte bones that store the step from the parent, or 132-byte bones that store the placed bone and the transform back to its parent.
    .ani8-byte header with 16-bit counts, or 20-byte header with 32-bit counts, a stated end and zero padding.
    Coverage: across all five games OmegaStudio reads 86 .trk, 86 .col, 115 .bpvs, 588 .zon, 103 .car, 64 .amd, 43 .ske and 348 .ani files, and each one re-serialises to the bytes it was read from. Monster Jam's 29 .col files are empty and its 29 .bpvs files are 12-byte stubs, so they hold nothing to read.

    In OmegaStudio

    • A .gda opens when its .gdf and .gdt sit beside it. The triple is also found inside a DS ROM and opens in place there; edits go back into the ROM when its rows can be written.
    • Every .rdf beside the archive is read for texture slots, and the Console reports how many databases it found.
    • Textures, tracks, scenery, collision, visibility, cars, characters, skeletons and clips each get their own row. Characters play every clip in their folder whose bone count matches their skeleton.
    • A texture is replaced from a PNG. Tracks, scenery, cars, characters, skeletons, clips and collision are replaced from glTF; Editing covers the rules.
    • Saving rebuilds the .gda and .gdt. The .gdf is only read.

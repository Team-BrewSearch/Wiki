Polarbit games use two audio formats: PSND sound blocks and PMOD tracker modules. CNK2 streams its music as PCM; CNK3D and Krazy Kart Racing play PMOD modules.

PSND

OffsetSizeTypeDescription
0x004char[4]Magic "PSND"
0x044u32Size of what follows
0x082u16A, the header bytes after this field; samples start at 10 + A
0x0A2u16Flags
0x0C2u16Sample rate, Hz
0x0Evar-Optional fields, in flag order
FlagField
0x001u8 channels (1 if absent)
0x002u8 bits per sample, 8 or 16; its presence means PCM
0x004u16 default volume, 0 to 256 (absent means full)
0x008u32 loop start, u32 loop end, in samples
0x010u8 loop type: 2 is ping-pong (absent means forward)
0x200u16 unknown, 80 to 180, always a multiple of 10
0x400i16 finetune, 1/128 semitone
0x800i8 relative note, semitones

No other flag bits occur. Without the bits field a sound is mono IMA ADPCM with no header of its own: the high nibble of each byte comes first, and both predictor and step index start at 0. 8-bit PCM is signed.

The unknown header words are identical in every module. The inflated body holds, in order: the order list (one byte per order), per-pattern u16 row counts when the rows field is 0, pattern cells (5 bytes per row per channel), the embedded PSND samples, and the instruments. Each instrument has a keyframe length, a 96-entry keymap, an envelope count and per-envelope points.

In OmegaStudio

  • PSND: only the flag bits above are accepted, the optional fields must end at 10 + A, bits must be 0, 8 or 16 and channels 1 or 2 (ADPCM mono only). The size word must match the file except in 16-bit PCM.
  • Sounds play in the Audio pane and export as WAV, with a smpl chunk carrying the loop when there is one.
  • Replacing from a WAV (integer PCM, 8 to 32 bits) needs the sound's own channel count and re-encodes in its own codec at the WAV's rate. Loop points are clamped to the new length, and ADPCM keeps the original nibbles wherever they still decode to the same samples.
  • PMOD: the size word and the 30 must match. Each embedded sample shows as a row that plays and can be replaced from a WAV the same way; the body is then re-compressed at level 9.

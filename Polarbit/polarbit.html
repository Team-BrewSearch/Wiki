Polarbit's mobile racing engine runs five iOS games: Crash Bandicoot Nitro Kart 2, Crash Bandicoot Nitro Kart 3D, Krazy Kart Racing, Reckless Getaway and Raging Thunder 2. All five pack their data into .vfs archives of one shared format. Every layout here is checked against OmegaStudio's Polarbit plugin.

    Conventions

    Byte order
    Little-endian, except the records of RT2 PLT plates
    Fixed
    Signed 32-bit 16.16 fixed point (value * 65536)
    GL constants
    Headers use OpenGL ES values directly: 4 GL_TRIANGLES, 5 GL_TRIANGLE_STRIP, 5121 GL_UNSIGNED_BYTE, 5123 GL_UNSIGNED_SHORT, 5132 GL_FIXED
    Texture V
    Runs upward from the bottom; OmegaStudio flips it for glTF

    Data Flow

    An entry is found by the hash of its path in a FUFS table, then unwrapped through zero, one or two zlib layers. What is left is identified by its first bytes:

    Starts withFormatExported as
    89 50 4E 47PNG.png
    34 00 00 00, "PVR!" at 44PVR.pvr
    PIFFPIFF model or track.pof, .trk
    4D 3C 2B 1APXC scene.pxc
    PSND, PMODSound, music module.psn, .pmod
    PFNTBitmap font.fnt
    LANG, FCOLString table.bin, .fcol
    TLPB, or a PLT-shaped headerAtlas plate.plt
    D0 CF 11 E0OLE compound file (Thumbs.db).db
    < after whitespace, or valid UTF-8XML or text.xml, .txt
    Coverage: the seven archives hold 2,511 entries. The four 2011 archives store 649 file names, and every one hashes to its table entry.

    In OmegaStudio

    • A .vfs opens when it passes the FUFS header check. Compression layers are removed on read and put back on write, so every row shows the real payload.
    • The older archives store no names, so the plugin recovers them from paths the data mentions (see FUFS); the Console reports how many it found.
    • PIFF models play their clips in the Animation pane. PIFF tracks and PXC cars, tracks and props render with their textures, found by path or, failing that, by file name.
    • Textures and fonts are replaced from PNG, sounds and PMOD samples from WAV, and PIFF models and PXC meshes from glTF. LANG and FCOL strings are edited in the Properties pane.
    • Saving rebuilds the archive in place. An entry that was not changed keeps its exact original bytes.

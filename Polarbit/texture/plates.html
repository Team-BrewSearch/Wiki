Plates map the sub-images packed into a texture atlas. Each plate sits next to a .pvr of the same name and size. Reckless Getaway and Raging Thunder 2 use different layouts.

TLPB (Getaway)

OffsetSizeTypeDescription
0x004char[4]Magic "TLPB"
0x044u324, version
0x084u32Atlas width
0x0C4u32Atlas height
0x1020u32[5]Unknown, small values (0/4/5, 0/1, 0/1, 1, 0/1)
0x244u32Entry count
0x284i32-1
0x2Cvarentry[]Entries, back to back

Entry

OffsetSizeTypeDescription
+0x0016fixed[4]u, v, du, dv as fractions of the atlas size
+0x104u16[2]Width and height in pixels, equal to du * W and dv * H
+0x144u320
+0x182u16Name length L, NUL included
+0x1ALchar[L]Name and NUL

Names are unique within a plate: TRACK_SELECT_BG, money1, sparks_blue_add, and the 573 glyph images of frontend/gray_plate.plt.

PLT (Raging Thunder 2)

RT2 plates have no magic and mix byte orders: a little-endian header, then big-endian records. They are the one exception to the little-endian rule.

OffsetSizeTypeDescription
0x002u16Atlas width W
0x022u16Atlas height H
0x042u16Rectangle count N
0x062u169
0x084u32W * H
0x0C4u320
0x104u321
0x1440 * Nrecord[N]Rectangles, big-endian

Rectangle (40 bytes, big-endian)

OffsetSizeTypeDescription
+0x008u32[2]Width and height in pixels
+0x0832fixed[4][2]u, v of the corners: top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right

The corners always form an axis-aligned rectangle whose size matches the pixel width and height. Rectangles have no names, so the game refers to them by index.

In OmegaStudio

  • A TLPB plate needs version 4 and the -1 at 0x28, and its entries must end exactly at the end of the file.
  • A payload is a PLT plate when its length is exactly 20 + 40 * N, the word at 0x06 is 9, and the word at 0x08 equals W * H.
  • The Properties pane lists the atlas size and every rectangle, named or numbered (#0, #1, ...), with its size and pixel position. Plates are read-only apart from raw byte replacement.
